Exploring the ICT Field

ICT is a very broad field and there are many different directions that someone can choose.

I am still exploring these areas and learning which one fits my interests and personality best.

Different Areas of ICT

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, systems and information from threats and attacks.

Software Development

Software development involves designing and creating programs and applications that solve problems.

Web Development

Web development focuses on creating websites and web applications using technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Data

Data-related ICT work involves collecting, organizing and analyzing information to discover useful patterns and insights.

Why Cybersecurity Interests Me

Recently cybersecurity has become more interesting to me because I like the idea of investigating problems and understanding what is happening behind a computer system.

I am interested in learning more about networks, security problems and how systems can be protected.

I originally thought web development might be something I wanted to focus on, but I discovered that website design can feel repetitive to me. I am more interested in technical problems and how systems work behind the scenes.

I am still early in my studies, so I want to continue exploring different fields before choosing one final direction.
