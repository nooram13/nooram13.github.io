Exploring the ICT Field

Before starting my ICT studies, I knew that technology was a very large field, but I did not really understand how many different jobs and directions exist inside ICT.

During my studies I started to discover that ICT is much more than programming. It includes areas such as software development, cybersecurity, data, web development, networking, cloud systems, databases and many other technical fields.

This is one of the things I find interesting about ICT. There is not only one possible career. A person can explore different subjects, develop different skills and later choose the direction that fits their interests and personality best.

At the moment I am still exploring these possibilities. I do not want to decide too early what my final career will be because I still have many subjects to experience during my studies.

Different Areas of ICT

Software Development

Software developers create applications and programs that solve problems or provide services to users.

A developer needs to understand programming languages, logic, problem solving and how different parts of a system work together.

During my studies I have already worked with programming and I know that it is an important part of ICT. I want to continue improving my programming skills because they can be useful in many different ICT careers.

Web Development

Web development is about creating websites and web applications. Front-end development focuses more on what users see and interact with, while back-end development focuses on the systems and logic behind the website.

During PCO I worked with HTML and CSS and created this portfolio website. This experience helped me understand how websites are structured and how CSS can be used to change layout, colours and design.

I enjoyed learning how websites work, but I also discovered that I am less interested in spending a lot of time changing visual details and page designs.

Data & Databases

Data is an important part of modern technology. Many organisations collect large amounts of information and need systems to store, organise and analyse that information.

People working with data can work with databases, data analysis or data science. Their work can help companies understand patterns, make decisions and improve their services.

I have not explored this area deeply yet, but I am interested in learning more about databases and understanding how information is stored and used by computer systems.

Networks & Systems

Networks allow computers and devices to communicate with each other. Almost every company depends on networks, servers and other systems to keep their technology working.

People who work with networks need to understand how devices communicate, how systems are connected and how problems can be found when something stops working.

I find this interesting because I like understanding what happens behind the screen and how different technical systems connect with each other.

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, systems and information against attacks, mistakes and other security problems.

Security professionals may investigate suspicious activity, find weaknesses in systems, monitor networks or help organisations make their technology safer.

Cybersecurity also requires constant learning because technology changes and new security problems appear all the time.

Cloud & IT Infrastructure

Many companies now use cloud services instead of keeping all their systems on one local computer or server.

ICT professionals working with cloud technology help organisations manage applications, storage and services through online platforms.

I think learning about infrastructure can also help me understand cybersecurity better because security depends on knowing how systems are built and connected.

Why Cybersecurity Interests Me

Out of the ICT fields I have discovered so far, cybersecurity is currently one of the areas that interests me the most.

One reason is that cybersecurity is not only about writing code. It also involves investigating problems, understanding computer systems, networks and user behaviour, and trying to understand why something happened.

I like the idea of looking at a technical problem and asking questions such as: What happened? Why did it happen? Where is the weakness? How could this problem be prevented next time?

I think this type of work matches my curiosity. I enjoy learning how things work behind the scenes instead of only looking at the final result on the screen.

Cybersecurity also seems interesting because there are many different directions inside the field. For example, someone can work with network security, security monitoring, penetration testing, digital forensics, cloud security or security analysis.

At this point I do not know which cybersecurity role would suit me best. I still need to learn much more about networking, operating systems, programming and security before I can make that decision.

What I Want to Learn

If I continue exploring cybersecurity, there are several skills that I would like to develop during my ICT studies.

  • Understanding computer networks
  • Learning more about operating systems
  • Improving my programming skills
  • Understanding databases and data
  • Learning how security problems are discovered
  • Understanding how systems can be protected
  • Improving my problem-solving skills
  • Learning how to work well in a technical team

I think having a broad ICT foundation is important before specialising. Understanding programming, data, networking and software development can all help someone become better at cybersecurity later.

What Kind of Work Fits Me?

I am beginning to understand that I prefer technical work where I can investigate something, solve a problem or understand how a system works.

I still enjoy creating things, but I think I am more interested in the technical side of ICT than in visual design.

I also like the idea that ICT professionals continue learning during their careers. Technology changes quickly, so there is always something new to understand.

For me, this is both challenging and exciting because I do not want a career where I stop learning after graduation.

My Future in ICT

I do not have one exact job title in mind yet, and I think that is normal because I am still at the beginning of my studies.

My current goal is to experience different ICT subjects and pay attention to which ones I enjoy the most.

At the moment cybersecurity is one of the directions I would like to explore further. In the future I would like to work on real technical problems, become more independent and continue developing my skills.

One of my long-term dreams is to work for a large technology company such as Microsoft. I know this would require a lot of knowledge and experience, but having a big goal gives me motivation to keep improving.

My Conclusion

Exploring the ICT field has helped me understand that choosing a career is not something I need to decide immediately.

I want to use my time at HZ to learn different technologies, improve my technical skills and discover what type of work suits me best.

Right now cybersecurity is the area that makes me the most curious, but I am keeping an open mind because I still have a lot to learn and experience.
