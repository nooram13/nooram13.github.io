MY CHOICE

Why I Chose ICT

Technology has always made me curious. I like understanding how computers, programs and systems work instead of only using them.

When I see a program or a computer system, I often become curious about what is happening behind the screen and how the different parts work together.

ICT interests me because it combines technology, problem solving and creativity. There is usually not only one possible solution to a problem, and I like trying different ways to find an answer.

Another reason I chose ICT is that technology keeps changing. I like the idea of having a career where I can continue learning new things instead of doing exactly the same thing forever.

What I Want to Learn

One of my main goals is to improve my programming skills. I want to become more confident when I receive a problem and need to turn my idea into working code.

I also want to understand computer systems, networks, databases and software better.

At the moment I am also interested in cybersecurity because I like investigating problems and understanding how systems can be protected.

My Expectations

I expected ICT to be challenging before I started the programme, and I have already experienced that some subjects require a lot of practice.

For example, programming can sometimes be difficult for me. However, I also see this as an opportunity to improve my problem-solving skills.

I want to learn by practising, making mistakes and understanding why something works or does not work.

Why I Chose the ICT Programme at HZ

I chose the ICT programme at HZ because I wanted a practical programme where I could learn by working on assignments and projects instead of only studying theory.

During the first part of the programme I have already worked with programming, computer science, HTML, CSS and Git. This has allowed me to experience different parts of ICT instead of focusing on only one subject.

This is important for me because I am still discovering which direction fits me best.

I also like that the programme is not only about technical knowledge. During projects I also need to communicate, work with other students, plan my work and learn how to ask for help when I need it.

These are skills I want to improve because working in ICT normally means working together with other people, not only working alone behind a computer.

My Long-Term Motivation

In the long term, I want to become an ICT professional who understands technology well and can solve real technical problems.

I do not know my exact future job yet. I think it is too early for me to choose one specific job because I am still learning about different ICT fields.

During my studies I want to explore Software Engineering, Data Science, cybersecurity, networks and other technical areas before making my final decision.

At the moment I feel more attracted to technical work where I can investigate something, understand how it works and solve a problem.

One of my long-term dreams is to work for a large technology company such as Microsoft.

I know that reaching a goal like this requires much more knowledge, experience and practice. For me, it is a long-term goal that motivates me to continue learning and improving.

My Goal

My goal during the ICT programme is to build a strong technical foundation and discover which area of ICT suits me best.

I also want to become more confident, independent and comfortable solving problems by myself.

By the end of my studies I want to be able to look back and see that I have improved both technically and personally.
