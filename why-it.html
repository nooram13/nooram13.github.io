MY CHOICE

Why I Chose ICT

Technology has always made me curious. I like understanding how computers, programs and systems work behind the scenes.

ICT gives me the opportunity to keep learning, solve problems and create things myself. That is one of the main reasons why this field interests me.

What I Want to Learn

I want to improve my programming skills and understand computers and networks better.

I am also becoming interested in cybersecurity because I like investigating problems and understanding how systems can be protected.

My Expectations

I expect ICT to be challenging, but I also see that as an opportunity to improve.

I want to learn by practicing, making mistakes and understanding why something works or does not work.

My Goal

My goal is to discover which area of ICT fits me best and develop the skills that I need for my future.

One of my dreams is to work for a company like Microsoft one day. Having a big goal motivates me to keep learning and not give up.
