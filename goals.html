My Goals & SWOT

Understanding my strengths and weaknesses helps me see what I need to improve during my studies.

I want to keep learning, become more confident and develop the skills that I need for a future career in ICT.

My SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • I am hardworking.
  • I do not give up easily.
  • I enjoy learning new things.
  • I am curious about technology.

Weaknesses

  • I sometimes become stressed when something is difficult.
  • I can be quiet when meeting new people.
  • I need more programming experience.
  • I sometimes doubt myself too quickly.

Opportunities

  • Learning different ICT subjects at HZ.
  • Working on new projects.
  • Meeting other ICT students.
  • Exploring areas such as cybersecurity.

Threats

  • Stress during exams.
  • Difficult programming assignments.
  • Losing confidence after mistakes.
  • Not managing my time well enough.

My Future Goal

My main goal is to keep developing my technical skills and discover the ICT direction that suits me best.

In the future I would like to work on meaningful technology projects, become independent and possibly work for a large technology company such as Microsoft.
