MY DEVELOPMENT ♡

My Goals & SWOT

I think it is important to understand both my strengths and the things that I still need to improve.

My goal is not only to pass my courses. I want to become more independent, improve my technical knowledge and develop skills that can help me in my future career.

My SWOT analysis helps me understand what can help me reach these goals and what could make studying more difficult for me.

My SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • I am hardworking.
  • I do not give up easily.
  • I enjoy learning new things.
  • I am curious about technology.
  • I am willing to practise when I do not understand something.

Weaknesses

  • I can become stressed during exams.
  • Stress sometimes makes me work more slowly.
  • I sometimes doubt myself when something is difficult.
  • I still need much more programming experience.
  • I can be quiet when working with new people.

Opportunities

  • Learning different ICT subjects at HZ.
  • Practising programming during assignments.
  • Working on projects with other students.
  • Asking teachers and classmates for help.
  • Exploring Software Engineering and Data Science.
  • Learning more about cybersecurity.

Threats

  • Becoming too stressed before an exam.
  • Falling behind when several assignments happen at the same time.
  • Spending too much time on one difficult problem.
  • Losing confidence after making mistakes.
  • Not planning my study time early enough.

What I Need to Study Successfully

My SWOT analysis shows that motivation alone is not enough. To complete my ICT studies successfully I also need to work on my weaknesses and use my strengths in a useful way.

Managing Stress

One of my biggest challenges is stress, especially during exams. When I become very stressed, I sometimes work more slowly and find it harder to think clearly.

To reduce this problem, I need to start preparing earlier and practise regularly instead of trying to learn everything just before an exam.

I also want to divide large tasks into smaller steps because finishing smaller tasks makes the work feel more manageable.

Improving My Programming

Programming is still one of the areas where I need the most practice.

Sometimes I understand what a question is asking but I need more time to decide how to write the solution in code.

Because my future goals are connected to technical ICT work, I need to continue practising programming regularly.

My goal is not only to finish programming assignments. I want to understand why my solution works so I can solve similar problems independently later.

Asking for Help

One thing I want to improve is asking for help earlier.

Sometimes I spend too much time trying to solve the same problem alone. Trying independently is important, but there is also a point where asking a teacher or another student can help me understand the problem faster.

Asking questions can also improve my communication skills and help me become more comfortable working with other people.

Time Management

Good planning is important because different courses can have assignments and exams during the same period.

I want to plan my study time so that I have enough time for programming practice, assignments and exam preparation.

Better time management can also reduce my stress because I will know what I need to do instead of feeling that everything needs to be finished at the same time.

Communication & Teamwork

ICT professionals often work in teams, so technical knowledge alone is not enough.

I want to become more confident when communicating my ideas, asking questions and participating in group projects.

This is important for my studies but also for my future career.

How My SWOT Connects to My Goals

My strengths can help me reach my professional goals. Being hardworking and willing to learn helps me continue even when a subject is difficult.

My curiosity also fits ICT because I enjoy understanding why something works and learning about new technology.

At the same time, my weaknesses show me what I need to improve. Stress, limited programming experience and lack of confidence can make studying more difficult if I do not work on them.

For this reason, my goals are not only technical. I also want to improve my planning, communication, confidence and ability to deal with difficult situations.

If I practise consistently, manage my time, ask for help when necessary and continue improving my technical skills, I believe I can complete the ICT programme successfully.

My Professional Goal

My main professional goal is to become a skilled ICT professional who can understand technical systems and solve problems independently.

At the moment I am especially interested in software, systems and cybersecurity, but I want to explore the different ICT directions before choosing one final career.

In the long term, I would like to work on meaningful technology projects and possibly work for a large technology company such as Microsoft.
