Finding My Direction

I am still at the beginning of my ICT studies, so I do not know exactly which job I want in the future.

I think this is something I will discover while learning about different areas of ICT.

What I Discovered

At first I thought web development could be interesting for me. However, after working with websites I discovered that design and repeatedly changing layouts are not my favorite parts of ICT.

I prefer understanding what is happening behind a system instead of mainly focusing on how something looks.

My Interest in Cybersecurity

Recently I have become more interested in cybersecurity.

I like learning how systems and networks work, finding problems and understanding how technology can be protected.

I am still exploring this field, so I do not want to decide too early that it will definitely be my future career.

Skills I Want to Improve

  • Programming
  • Problem solving
  • Networking knowledge
  • Cybersecurity knowledge
  • Communication
  • Confidence

My Dream

One of my dreams is to work for Microsoft one day.

I know that this is a big goal and requires a lot of learning and experience, but having a big goal gives me motivation to keep going.

My Next Step

For now, my goal is to continue learning different ICT subjects and discover which direction fits me best.
