MY FUTURE ♡

Finding My Direction in ICT

I am still at the beginning of my ICT studies, so I do not want to choose one final career too early.

During the programme I have the opportunity to learn about different areas of ICT and discover which type of work fits my interests and personality.

Two important directions in the programme are Software Engineering and Data Science.

I wanted to understand both directions better before deciding which one currently interests me more.

Software Engineering

Software Engineering is about designing, developing, testing and maintaining software.

It is not only about writing code. Software engineers also need to understand what users or clients need, think about the structure of a program, test their solutions and improve software when problems are found.

Another important part of Software Engineering is maintainability. Code should not only work once. It should also be understandable enough that other developers can work with it later.

Software engineers often work in teams. This means they also need skills such as communication, planning, Git and teamwork.

During my studies I have already experienced a small part of this through programming assignments and creating my portfolio website. I have learned that even a simple program can require planning, testing and correcting mistakes.

I find the problem-solving side of Software Engineering interesting. I like the idea of receiving a technical problem and trying to understand how I can solve it.

I still need much more programming experience before I could call myself a software developer, but improving these skills is one of my main goals during the programme.

Data Science

Data Science focuses on using data to discover useful information, patterns and relationships.

Many organisations collect large amounts of information. Data scientists can analyse this information to answer questions and help organisations make better decisions.

Data Science can involve programming, databases, mathematics and statistics.

A Data Scientist may first need to collect or clean the data before it can be analysed. After that, different methods can be used to find patterns or make predictions.

For example, data could be used to understand customer behaviour, recognise unusual activity, compare results or predict what might happen in the future.

Programming languages such as Python are often useful in Data Science. This is interesting to me because I already had some experience with Python before starting at HZ.

I like the investigation side of Data Science. You start with information and try to understand what that information is telling you.

However, I currently have less experience with statistics and analysing data than I have with programming.

Because of this, I need to experience Data Science more during my studies before I know whether it could be the right direction for me.

Software Engineering or Data Science?

Both directions have parts that I find interesting.

Data Science interests me because I like the idea of investigating information and finding patterns that are not immediately obvious.

Software Engineering interests me because it is more directly connected to building systems, programming and solving technical problems.

At this moment I think Software Engineering is slightly closer to my interests.

I enjoy understanding how computer systems and software work behind the scenes, and I want to become better at programming and technical problem solving.

This does not mean that I have already made my final choice. I think it is important to experience both directions during the programme before deciding.

My opinion may change when I gain more experience with real Software Engineering and Data Science projects.

My Interest in Cybersecurity

Another area that currently interests me is cybersecurity.

I like the idea of investigating technical problems and trying to understand what happened, why it happened and how the same problem could be prevented in the future.

Cybersecurity is also connected to Software Engineering. To understand security problems, I first need to understand software, computer systems and networks.

Because of this, Software Engineering could give me a useful technical foundation if I later decide to move towards cybersecurity.

Cybersecurity itself also has many different jobs. Some professionals work with network security, monitoring, penetration testing, digital forensics or cloud security.

I do not yet know which of these areas would fit me best. I first want to build a strong ICT foundation.

Skills I Need for My Future

No matter which direction I choose, I think there are several skills that will be useful.

  • Programming and logical thinking
  • Problem solving
  • Understanding computer systems
  • Understanding networks
  • Working with data and databases
  • Testing and finding mistakes
  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Time management
  • Continuing to learn new technology

My Long-Term Direction

My current preference is towards Software Engineering and other technical areas that involve systems, programming and problem solving.

Cybersecurity is also something I would like to explore further because it combines several subjects that interest me.

Data Science is currently less familiar to me, but I want to keep an open mind until I have worked with it more during the programme.

My long-term goal is not simply to choose a job title. I want to develop enough knowledge that I can solve real problems independently and continue learning throughout my career.

One of my dreams is to work for a large technology company such as Microsoft one day.

I know this would require many years of learning and experience, but it gives me something to work towards.

My Conclusion

At this point, Software Engineering seems closer to my interests than Data Science because I enjoy programming, technical systems and problem solving.

However, I am still at the beginning of the programme and I want to experience both directions before making my final decision.

For now, my goal is to build a strong foundation in programming, software, data and computer systems and use that knowledge to discover the ICT career that suits me best.
